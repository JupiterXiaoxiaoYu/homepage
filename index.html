About Me

Hello! I'm Jupiter. I am a Software Developer with an interest in Web3, Blockchain and Artificial Intelligence. I am currently in my 3rd year at Fudan University for a whole-year exchange program as a student of The University of Edinburgh, persuing a Cognitive Science degree with a focus on Computer Science and Engineering.

Besides and more specifically, My interests range from machine learning, natural language processing, multi-modal learning and generating (cognition guided), reinforcement learning (multi-agent learning), federated Learning (privacy computing), blockchain application, quantitative trading of cryptocurrency, metaverse, and so on.

Now, I'm the founding member and front-end engineer of Rosen Bridge (ROSEN), which is a Web3 startup company in the intersection of GameFi and SocialFi. I'm also the associate of QAQ (Quadratic Acceleration Quantum) focused on helping entrepreneurs to build their startups better. If you are currently looking for web3 projects, either for investment or job hunting, don't hesitate to contact me!
